# References

This directory records external expository references used while developing the project. To keep
the repository small and avoid redistributing third-party documents without an explicit license,
the references are linked to their original hosts rather than copied into the repository.

- Connor Lockhart, *The Baldwin-Lachlan proof of Morley's theorem* — University of Chicago REU
  2020: https://math.uchicago.edu/~may/REU2020/REUPapers/Lockhart.pdf
- Nick Ramsey, *Morley's Categoricity Theorem* — University of Chicago VIGRE/REU:
  https://math.uchicago.edu/~may/VIGRE/VIGRE2010/REUPapers/RamseyN.pdf

These links point to documents hosted by the University of Chicago Mathematics Department. The
copyright and redistribution terms of those documents remain with their respective authors and
publishers/hosts.
